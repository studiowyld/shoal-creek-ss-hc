Our vision

Every member comes ready to share what they’re learning, so all are edified, testimonies grow, and everyone takes the Spirit home.

The thesis

“If there is a culture shift toward an emphasis on coming to share rather than coming to learn, then that will positively affect the conversion of the members of the wards as well as improve the quality and frequency of ministering.”
